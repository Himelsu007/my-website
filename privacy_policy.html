PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This page explains what personal data Locked In Lx collects when you sign up for a run or a training session, order from the store or visit lockedinlx.com: why we collect it, who else handles it, how long we keep it, and what you can ask us to do with it.

1Who is responsible

Locked In Lx, Lisbon, Portugal, is responsible for your data (the “controller”). For anything on this page, write to support@lockedinlx.com or message us on WhatsApp.

2What we collect and why

We don’t sell your data, and we don’t use it for advertising. We only ask for an age group, never a date of birth. Players under 18 need a parent or guardian’s authorisation (see the Terms of Participation).

3Payments

Nothing is paid on this website. You pay by MB WAY, bank transfer or cash, as agreed with us on WhatsApp. Your bank or MB WAY handles those payments; we only see your name, the amount and the reference.

4Who else handles it

We use a few services to run Locked In Lx. They handle your data for us, under their own security and privacy terms:

  • Google (Google Sheets and Apps Script, where sign-ups and orders are kept; Gmail, which sends our order emails; Google Analytics, only if you accept cookies).
  • Apple (iCloud Mail, our support@lockedinlx.com mailbox).
  • WhatsApp (Meta), when you message us or a sign-up or order opens WhatsApp.
  • GitHub (Microsoft), which hosts this website and so sees technical data such as your IP address when you load a page.
  • Trustpilot, whose reviews widget appears on the home page.

We also keep orders, customers and invoices in our own records app on our computer. It isn’t shared with anyone.

5Data outside the EU

Google, Apple, Meta and Microsoft may process data in the United States. They take part in the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and use the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses, which the GDPR accepts as safeguards for those transfers.

6How long we keep it

  • Sign-ups, rosters, training bookings, orders, delivery details, order emails and waivers: 12 months after the run, session or order, then deleted.
  • Invoices and accounting records: 10 years, because Portuguese tax law requires it.
  • Order links: they stop working once the order is delivered or cancelled, or 18 days after it was placed, whichever comes first.
  • Leaderboard: for the season shown, unless you ask us to take you off sooner.
  • WhatsApp chats: until either of us deletes them.

7Your order link

When you order, you get a link to an order page. The order details travel inside that link and are opened by your browser, so keep it to yourself: anyone you send it to can see your order and delivery address. The short version of the link only works with a private key that only you have, and every order link stops working on the rules in section 6.

8What stays on your device

Your bag, the delivery or pickup details and email you type into it, and the runs you’ve signed up for are saved in your own browser, so they’re still there if you reload the page. They aren’t sent anywhere until you send an order or a sign-up. Empty the bag or clear your browser’s data to remove them. The Cookie Policy lists them.

9Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to:

  • show you the personal data we have about you, or give you a copy to take elsewhere;
  • correct it, or delete it;
  • stop or limit using it, including leaving the leaderboard (or appearing by your initials only) and not featuring you in photos or videos;
  • stop emailing you: withdrawing your consent never affects what was done before.

Write to support@lockedinlx.com or message us on WhatsApp. We answer within one month. If you think we’ve handled your data wrongly, you can complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, the CNPD (www.cnpd.pt).

10Keeping it safe

Your data sits in our own Google and Apple accounts and on our own computer, and only we can open them. Order links expire, and the short ones need a private key. No system is perfectly secure; if something ever went wrong in a way that put you at risk, we would tell you and the CNPD as the law requires.

11Changes

If we change how we use your data, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
