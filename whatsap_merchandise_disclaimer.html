STORE TERMS

Last updated: 3 November 2026

How buying from the Locked In Lx store works: ordering, paying, delivery, and what happens if something isn’t right.

1Who you’re buying from

Locked In Lx, Lisbon, Portugal. support@lockedinlx.com or WhatsApp.

2Ordering

  • Add what you want to the bag, choose delivery or pickup, and tap Order on WhatsApp. That sends us your order as a request: nothing is charged on the website.
  • We check that everything is in stock and confirm with you on WhatsApp. The order is agreed once we confirm it.
  • If something turns out to be out of stock, we tell you and you pay nothing for it.

3Prices and payment

Prices are in euros and are what you pay. Delivery in Lisbon and pickup at a run are free. You pay by MB WAY, bank transfer or cash, on delivery or at pickup.

4Delivery and pickup

  • Delivery: free express delivery anywhere in Lisbon. Ordered before 18:00, it usually arrives the same evening; later orders usually arrive the next day. We confirm the time on WhatsApp.
  • Pickup: free, at the run you choose. Show the code on your order page and we hand it over.
  • If we can’t deliver when we said, we’ll tell you and agree a new time, or cancel and refund you if you prefer.

5Your order page and updates

Your order comes with a link to an order page that shows its progress. Keep the link to yourself: anyone you send it to can see your order and address. It stops working once the order is delivered or cancelled, or 18 days after you ordered. If you left an email in the bag, we may email you updates about the order.

6Returns: 30 days

  • Not the right size, or not for you? Message us within 30 days of receiving it, and we’ll swap it or refund you.
  • Please return items unworn and in the condition you received them. We’ll agree on WhatsApp how to get it back to us: at a run, or collected in Lisbon.
  • We refund within 14 days of getting the item back, the same way you paid.

7Your rights under the law

On top of our 30-day returns, Portuguese law gives you:

  • 14 days to change your mind on anything ordered at a distance, without giving a reason (the right of withdrawal, Decree-Law 24/2014). Our 30-day returns already cover this.
  • A 3-year guarantee that what you buy matches its description and works as it should (Decree-Law 84/2021). If it doesn’t, we repair, replace or refund it.

8Problems and complaints

Tell us first: most things are fixed with a message. If we can’t agree, you can turn to a consumer arbitration centre, such as the Centro de Arbitragem de Conflitos de Consumo de Lisboa (www.centroarbitragemlisboa.pt).

9Your data

What we keep about your order, and for how long, is in the Privacy Policy.
