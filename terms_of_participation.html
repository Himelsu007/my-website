TERMS OF PARTICIPATION

Last updated: 3 November 2026

These terms apply to the pickup runs and the training sessions Locked In Lx organises in Lisbon. Booking a spot or a session means you accept them.

1What we organise

  • Pickup runs: organised games. The price of each run (shown on the run) covers the court and running costs.
  • Training: paid one-to-one sessions. The packs and prices are on the Training page.

Locked In Lx, Lisbon, Portugal. support@lockedinlx.com or WhatsApp.

2Who can take part

  • You must be physically able to play basketball safely, and tell us before playing about any injury or condition that could put you or others at risk.
  • Players under 18 need a parent or legal guardian’s authorisation, given through the Injury Waiver.

3Booking and paying

You book through the website, which sends your booking to us on WhatsApp. Your spot or session is confirmed when we confirm it there. You pay by MB WAY, bank transfer or cash, as agreed with us.

4Cancellations and refunds

For runs and training sessions alike:

  • You cancel at least 2 hours before the start: full refund, or we move you to another session if you prefer.
  • You cancel less than 2 hours before the start: we refund 50%.
  • We cancel the session: full refund.

5Risks and responsibility

Basketball is a contact sport. Sprains, fractures, cuts, collisions and falls can happen even when everyone plays fairly. You take part voluntarily and accept those normal risks of the game.

Locked In Lx is not responsible for injuries that come from the normal risks of playing. We don’t provide medical cover or accident insurance for players, so you may want your own. Nothing in these terms limits our responsibility where the law doesn’t allow it, including for death or personal injury caused by our fault, or for damage we cause on purpose or through gross negligence.

6Behaviour

To keep the runs fair and safe, we can stop anyone from playing, without a refund, for:

  • dangerous play;
  • aggressive or abusive behaviour;
  • disrupting the session;
  • not respecting other players or the game rules.

7Photos and video

We sometimes film or photograph sessions and share them on our website and social media. If you’d rather not appear, tell us before the session or at any time after it: we won’t feature you, and we’ll take down posts where you can be recognised.

8Leaderboard

Results from the runs feed the leaderboard on the home page, which shows players’ names and win rates. Ask us and we’ll show you by your initials, or take you off.

9Your data and changes

How we handle your data is in the Privacy Policy. If these terms change, we’ll update this page and the date at the top; a booking follows the terms in force when it was made.
