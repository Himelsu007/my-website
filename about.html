ABOUT US

Locked In Lx is a pick up basketball community in Lisbon. We run organised indoor games for players who want real runs: full court, balanced teams and a group that shows up to compete.

Pick up basketball in Lisbon, done properly

No more hunting for an outdoor court with a bent rim, or waiting around until enough people turn up. Book your spot, show up, play.

How a run works

Most runs are full court 5v5 at Pavilhão Islâmico de Lisboa, an indoor court, so the weather never cancels a game. Each run has 20 spots and costs €5.

Teams rotate so everyone gets minutes, and every format (4v4, and 5v5 with three or four teams) is written down in our game rules, so you know how the night works before you arrive.

Who plays

The level is semi-competitive to high intensity. You'll play with locals, students and expats from all over the world: players who came up through clubs and players who learned on the playground. What they share is that they take the game seriously.

Every run counts towards the season leaderboard, rated on skill, consistency and impact.
